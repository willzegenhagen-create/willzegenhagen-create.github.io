Common questions

How do I restore Remove ads?

Use the Google Play account that made the purchase, connect to the internet and select Restore purchases in Orbit's Remove ads screen. The purchase is a one-time upgrade, not a subscription.

Are satellite positions live?

No. Orbit uses a saved public catalogue. Last updated refers to its snapshot date; orbital elements may be older. Pointing values and inclined paths are estimates. Verify current authorised data and equipment procedures before use.

Does Orbit track my location?

Device location is requested only when you choose it. Orbit does not track you in the background. Maps and address search use online providers; see the privacy policy for the information those services receive.

How do I remove saved information?

You can change your saved location and selections in the app, or clear Orbit's storage in Android settings. This does not delete Google Play transaction records or provider logs. Email us to request deletion of support correspondence.

Built for reference

Orbit is intended for ages 13 and over. It does not control antenna hardware, grant permission to transmit or replace current operational guidance. Do not rely on it alone for navigation or safety-critical decisions.

Maps: OpenFreeMap, OpenMapTiles and OpenStreetMap contributors. Satellite catalogue: CelesTrak. Address search: Photon.
