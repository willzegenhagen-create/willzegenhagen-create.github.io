Orbit — Satellite Finder

Privacy and support · ZiggyDev · Last updated 30 October 2026

Who operates Orbit

Orbit is provided by ZiggyDev. Contact us for app support or privacy requests at wziggydev@gmail.com.

Your location and saved settings

You may enter coordinates, search for an address, or request your device location. Device location is requested only when you choose that feature; Orbit does not track you in the background. The app saves your selected location, satellite selections and display preferences on your device. You can replace them in the app or remove local app data through Android settings or by uninstalling.

Maps and address search

Maps are downloaded from OpenFreeMap. Map requests include the displayed area, your IP address and technical request information; the displayed area may correspond to your location. See OpenFreeMap's privacy policy.

As you type in address search, your query and IP address are sent to the public Photon service operated by komoot to obtain suggestions. Search text may include an address or other location information. Use manual coordinates instead if you do not wish to send address queries. See Photon and komoot's privacy information. Providers may process technical logs under their own policies; deleting the app does not delete those logs.

Advertising and choices

The monetised Android release uses Google AdMob for banner advertising. Google may process IP-derived approximate location, device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic data for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Orbit requests non-personalised ads, which can still require data processing and consent. Where required, a consent message is shown before ads are requested. Available privacy choices can be reopened in Settings. See Google advertising information and Google's privacy policy.

Optional purchase

Google Play processes the one-time Remove ads purchase. ZiggyDev does not receive your card details. Orbit receives signed purchase information and stores a receipt locally to check ad-free access, including offline. Restoring purchases checks the Google Play account on the device. Refunds and revoked purchases may update access after the next successful store check. Google retains transaction records according to its policies.

Retention and support requests

Local settings remain until replaced or cleared. Purchase receipts remain locally while needed to establish entitlement. Third-party logs and payment records have provider-controlled retention. If you contact ZiggyDev, your email address and message are used to respond to your request. Contact wziggydev@gmail.com for access, correction or deletion requests concerning support information. Resolved support emails are deleted after 12 months, except where needed to meet legal obligations or handle ongoing disputes.

Security and international processing

Online services use encrypted connections. Providers may process data outside your country. No system can guarantee absolute security. Orbit has no ZiggyDev login account and no developer-operated tracking server.

Age range and advertising

Orbit asks for an age range, not a birth date. The range is saved only on your device and can be changed in Settings. Advertising stays off until a range is selected and for all users under 18. Orbit does not request an advertising consent form or banner ads for those users. Adults use Google’s consent flow where required. The optional purchase is not needed to turn off ads for under-18 users; it also adds the quick reference strip.

Audience

Orbit is a satellite reference utility intended for teenagers aged 13 and over and adults. It is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to ZiggyDev, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Satellite information

Satellite information is a bundled public catalogue snapshot, not live tracking. Last updated refers to the catalogue date, and the source orbital elements may be older. Inclined paths and pointing results are estimates. Verify current authorised data and equipment procedures. Orbit is not a sole source for navigation, precise alignment or safety-critical work, and does not grant permission to transmit.

This support website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request data, including IP addresses, under its privacy statement. ZiggyDev has not added website analytics or tracking cookies.

Changes

This page will be updated when the app's privacy practices change. Contact ZiggyDev through the support address above with questions.
